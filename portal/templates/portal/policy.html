Source & Publishing Policy

How SarkariNaukri discovers, verifies and publishes recruitment information.

Core rule: a competitor/job-alert portal may be used as a discovery signal only. SarkariNaukri does not publish that portal's article body as its own. A job must resolve to an official recruiting-authority source before automatic publication.

Official-source first

Our automation extracts factual fields from official recruitment notices and stores the official notification URL, source hash and verification timestamp. Users are directed to the official authority for the final application.

Third-party discovery

Optional discovery adapters retain only the minimum signal needed to identify a possible vacancy, such as a headline and URL. They are disabled by default and must respect the source's current robots instructions, terms and copyright policy.

Automatic publishing gate

Only high-confidence records with an official source and a validated future deadline are eligible for automatic job publication. Conflicts, missing dates and weak extraction go to staff review.

Corrections

When a source hash changes, the pipeline re-extracts the record, stores field-level changes and updates the published page when validation succeeds.

Independence

SarkariNaukri is an independent information portal and is not a government website. Official recruitment notifications remain authoritative.
