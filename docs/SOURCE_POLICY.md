# Source compliance policy

- Official recruiting-authority notices are the publication source of truth.
- Third-party job portals may be used only as optional discovery signals when their current robots instructions and terms permit the intended access.
- Do not store or republish competitor article bodies. Do not use "AI paraphrasing" as a substitute for source permission.
- Prefer extracting facts (dates, vacancy counts, eligibility, official links) from the official notice and generate an original neutral summary.
- Preserve the official notification URL, verification time, and a source-content hash for auditability.
- Respect takedown/correction requests and update source rules when policies change.
- This code cannot guarantee legal clearance for a brand, domain, source, or content use. Obtain local legal review for launch-critical questions.
